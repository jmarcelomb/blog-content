This domain's owner operates a private, personal-use OAuth client
("Hermes Agent") that connects only to the owner's own Google account
(Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Sheets and Docs) for personal
automation.

  personal devices. No third party receives them.
- No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.
- Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's
  [Google Account permissions page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
